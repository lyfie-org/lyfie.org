Luthor is a rich-text editor ecosystem for teams that want a modern developer
experience without editor lock-in. It ships as two packages, so you can start
fast and go deep later.

## Two packages, one editor

- **`@lyfie/luthor`** — plug-and-play presets with a polished UI, for shipping
  quickly with almost no setup.
- **`@lyfie/luthor-headless`** — the typed extension runtime underneath, for
  when your product needs full control of every pixel.

## Why it exists

Editors are where products quietly lock people in: proprietary formats, paid
tiers for basic features, UI you cannot change. Luthor is MIT-licensed, built on
Meta's [Lexical](https://lexical.dev), works with React, Next.js, Astro, Vite and
Remix, and round-trips Markdown losslessly.

It is also the editor inside [Papyra](/projects/papyra/) — Lyfie builds on its
own tools.
